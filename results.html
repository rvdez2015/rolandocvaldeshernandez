NEA moderation: evidence of assessment practice

The school’s 2026 review of A Level NEA moderation records clerically accurate marks broadly in line with the national standard. It also highlights imaginative solutions, candidate effort and source referencing.

This is a paraphrase of the school’s review of moderation, rather than a direct quotation from the original OCR moderator report.

How the review informs teaching

  • Track theory, programming and project development separately to identify specific needs.
  • Use regular programming practice and timed questions to check readiness.
  • Build in early project milestones and attention to documentation and referencing.
