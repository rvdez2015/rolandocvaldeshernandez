Curriculum progression

A coherent Computer Science pathway across Years 5–13, adapted to learners’ prior knowledge.

KS2 · Years 5–6

Digital literacy, safe technology use, documents, presentations, spreadsheets, search, copyright, web creation and physical computing.

KS3 · Years 7–9

Computer systems, networks, cybersecurity, binary and media representation, HTML, App Lab, programming and responsible AI use.

KS4 · OCR J277

Computer systems, memory and storage, networks, security, system software and wider impacts; algorithms, programming, robust programs, Boolean logic and languages.

KS5 · OCR H446 / H046

Computer architecture, software, data exchange, data types and structures, legal and ethical issues, computational thinking, programming and algorithms. H446 adds the programming project.

Planning and assessment

Planning combines clear sequencing, retrieval of prior learning, explicit modelling and practical application. Formative checks and summative assessments identify misconceptions and inform intervention. Examination analysis feeds back into curriculum priorities.
