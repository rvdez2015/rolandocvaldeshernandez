Embedded Systems: Purpose and Characteristics

Year 10 · OCR J277 · 1.1.3 · 45 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Define an embedded system accurately.
  • Identify typical characteristics of embedded systems.
  • Distinguish embedded systems from general-purpose computers.
  • Apply the definition to unfamiliar real-world examples.

Success criteria

  • I can explain the dedicated function of an embedded system.
  • I can describe at least three typical characteristics, with suitable qualifications.
  • I can justify whether a device contains an embedded system using evidence.

Activity: Classify the device
