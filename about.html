Professional Identity

I see Computer Science as a progression from confident and responsible use of technology to increasingly sophisticated computational thinking, programming and problem solving. I aim to build both technical competence and intellectual independence.

Alongside classroom teaching, I develop curriculum resources, assessment systems and Project X — a teaching intelligence platform connecting curriculum, assessment, revision and professional evidence.

Teaching Approach

My teaching uses explicit explanation, modelling, retrieval practice, adaptive support and purposeful assessment. I use assessment evidence to identify misconceptions, target intervention and adjust subsequent teaching, while maintaining high expectations for technical vocabulary, reasoning and independent problem solving.
