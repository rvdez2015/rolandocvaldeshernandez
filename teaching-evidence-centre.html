What to look for

  • Subject knowledge: accurate use of binary vocabulary and worked calculations.
  • Sequencing: movement from explanation to modelling and application.
  • Assessment: opportunities to check understanding before the next step.
  • Inclusion: scaffolding and explanation that help pupils access the task.

These are viewing prompts, not time-coded assessor judgments.

Reflection and next steps

My development priorities include chunking longer modelling into shorter episodes, building in pupil activity and checking vocabulary and prior knowledge. Scaffolds should support access to the most challenging outcomes while gradually increasing independence.
